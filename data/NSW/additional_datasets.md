# Potential Datasets
**Bureau of Meteorology**
- https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/austmaps/metadata-daily-solar-exposure.shtml - Gridded Daily Solar Exposure Dataset
    - This dataset calculates solar irradiance by measuring the absorption of atmospheric water vapour and cloud albedo reflection from space
- https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/data/stations/about-weather-station-data.shtml - Half-Hourly Weather Station Data
    - This dataset captures high-resolution, localised weather parameters including temperature, humidity, and critical visual estimations of cloud cover measured in oktas.

**Solcast API**
- https://docs.solcast.com.au
    - Primarily interested in cloud coverage data though has extensive data on live and historical solar irradiance, PV power, and weather data.

**APVI Localised Solar Capacity Dataset**
- https://data.maitland.nsw.gov.au/explore/dataset/solar-energy-australian-pv-institute/information/
    - This is a detailed dataset tracking the number of solar installations and total capacity (in kW) broken down by specific localities and Local Government Areas (LGAs) within NSW.
 
**NEMOSIS: NEM Open Source Information Service**
- https://github.com/UNSW-CEEM/NEMOSIS
    - Contains estimates of household rooftop solar generation.
